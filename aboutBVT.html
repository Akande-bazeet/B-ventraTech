About us

Who We Are

B-Ventra Tech is an IT solutions, design and branding studio helping students and small businesses bring their ideas to life — online and on screen.

Our story

Ideas, designs and solutions — under one roof

We started B-Ventra Tech to solve a simple problem: students and small businesses need reliable, affordable help with tech and design work, but don't know where to turn.

Today we combine web development, graphic design, video editing and technical writing into one service — so you can get everything done in one place, on time, every time.

What we bring to the table

✅ Professional web development — HTML5, CSS3, Tailwind CSS & responsive design

✅ Java & object-oriented software development

✅ Professional brand design — flyers, social graphics & identity systems

✅ Professional video editing for short-form & social content

✅ Technical documentation & SIWES/IT industrial training reports

✅ AI-assisted development for faster, more efficient delivery
